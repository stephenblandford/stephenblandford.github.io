## About

I am a PhD student in Operations Research and Industrial Engineering
at The University of Texas at Austin. My research uses optimization
to help decision makers build more resilient electric power systems.

I am particularly interested in how we can restore electricity after
disasters and prioritize infrastructure investments to reduce the
impacts of future disruptions.

[Email](mailto:stephenblandford@utexas.edu) · [CV](./cv.pdf) · [GitHub](https://github.com/stephenblandford)

## Research

### Power grid restoration

Developing optimization models to coordinate repairs to transmission
and distribution systems and restore electricity following disasters.

### Infrastructure hardening

Estimating the economic benefits of investments that reduce power
outages and their impacts on communities and businesses.

### Power system interdiction

Studying how defensive investments and distributed generation can
improve the resilience of electrical distribution systems.
